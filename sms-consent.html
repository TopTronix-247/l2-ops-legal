Terence Junious SMS Verbal Consent

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This page is the public record of how Terence Junious obtains consent before sending service text messages. It is maintained so a reviewer can see the full verbal script and the process staff follow.

Who sends the messages

Texts are sent by Terence Junious from our business text number, using the L2 OPS app. They are service messages only. We do not send marketing or promotional texts.

What the messages are for

If you opt in, you may receive service-related texts about your scheduled field service work, such as:

  • appointment confirmations
  • technician arrival updates
  • completion notices
  • replies to your questions about a job

Where we ask

Consent is requested verbally, either in person or by phone, when you request or schedule service. There is no website form and no text-keyword signup. We do not treat a reply to an unsolicited first text as consent.

Exact script staff read

Before any service text is sent to a new number, staff read this script (inserting their name and the mobile number being discussed):

Hi, this is [name] with Terence Junious field service. If you'd like, I can send you service text messages about your field service work: appointment confirmations, technician arrival updates, completion notices, and replies to your questions. Message frequency varies, typically a few messages per job. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Our Terms are at https://toptronix-247.github.io/l2-ops-legal/terms.html and our Privacy Policy is at https://toptronix-247.github.io/l2-ops-legal/privacy.html. May I text you at [mobile number] for these service updates?

What counts as a yes

Only a clear, affirmative spoken yes counts as consent. Examples: "Yes," "Yes, you can text me," or "That's fine, go ahead and text me."

Silence, "maybe," "I don't know," "not right now," or "no" means no. We will not send service texts to that number.

How each answer is recorded

After a clear yes, staff record the person's name, the mobile number they agreed to, the date and time, and the words "verbal yes" in a private consent log kept by Terence Junious. If the person does not agree, staff record that no consent was given and no service texts are sent to that number. Individual consent records are kept private and are not posted on this page.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your service activity. You will typically receive a few messages per scheduled job, and only when there is a service update.

Message and data rates may apply.

STOP and HELP

  • Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive one opt-out confirmation and no further service texts.
  • Reply START after that only if you want to resubscribe. START is not how new customers opt in.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email terencejunious83@gmail.com.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the service providers needed to deliver the messages (such as Twilio).
