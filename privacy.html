L2 OPS Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

L2 OPS (package com.l2.ops) is a field-operations app for kiosk maintenance, work orders, inventory, invoicing, calendar, and related job tools. It is operated by Terence Junious. Contact: terencejunious83@gmail.com.

Who this app is for

L2 OPS is a business tool for adult technicians and operators. It is not directed at children.

Information we collect

Depending on which features you use, L2 OPS may collect:

  • Account information such as name and email, used to sign you in and keep your data associated with your account.
  • Work data you enter such as work orders, inventory, invoices, customers, notes, and archives. This is stored so the app can run your operations.
  • Calendar data if you connect Google Calendar, so the app can show and manage job schedules.
  • Photos, videos, and camera capture when you take or select images for work orders, inventory, receipts, barcodes, or document intake. Images may be processed on-device (including on-device Vision/OCR) and uploaded to your operations backend when you save them.
  • Approximate or precise location when you grant location permission, used for route/ETA and job arrival. Location is not sold and is not used for ads.
  • Device and app information needed to run the app, such as online/offline state and crash/debug logs you trigger.

How we use information

We use this information to provide L2 OPS features: sign-in, job tracking, inventory, invoicing, calendar sync, navigation, photo evidence, and support. We do not sell personal information. We do not use the Android Advertising ID. The app does not contain ads.

Where information is stored

Account and operations data are stored on Google Cloud services used by L2 OPS (including Cloud Run backends). If you connect Google Calendar, Google processes that calendar data under Google’s terms as well. Some capture and reminders can be stored locally on your device until they sync.

Sharing

We share data with service providers only as needed to operate the app (hosting, email delivery of invoices you send, and Google services you connect). We may disclose information if required by law. We do not share data with advertisers.

Your choices

You can deny camera or location permissions in Android settings. Features that need those permissions will not work without them. To request access, correction, or deletion of account data, email terencejunious83@gmail.com.

Children

L2 OPS is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of L2 OPS after an update means you accept the revised policy.
